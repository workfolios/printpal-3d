# Phase 3D Slice Five Scope Control

## Included

- Outcome Capture route and screen.
- User-authored Outcome persistence.
- Project-bound success-criteria results.
- Outcome lifecycle transitions.
- Attempt duplication service and transaction.
- Parent-Attempt relationship.
- Inherited and changed field indicators.
- Explicit planned-change capture.
- Failure Review documentation prompts.
- Attempt History selection.
- Unit, persistence, build, desktop, and mobile validation.

## Excluded

- Comparison record creation.
- Constant and change classification.
- Outcome-difference comparison.
- Causal claims.
- AI interpretation.
- Next-Test Plan creation.
- Export or import.
- Backup or restore.
- Authentication, cloud sync, hosted storage, external database, or deployment.

## Duplication Rule

A duplicated Attempt inherits applicable context and source references, but it does not inherit the source Attempt's Plan, Outcome, comparison links, next-test reference, completion date, identifier, attempt number, or timestamps.
